Pravila privatnosti / Privacy policy

Aplikaciju "Home Assistant" koristi samo vlasnik za pristup vlastitom Google kalendaru iz osobnog Home Assistanta. Podaci iz kalendara čitaju se i upisuju samo na vlasnikovom kućnom poslužitelju i ne dijele se ni s kim.

This app is used only by its owner to access his own Google Calendar from a personal Home Assistant installation. Calendar data is read and written only on the owner's home server and is not shared with anyone.
